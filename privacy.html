Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. This policy describes the AutoClip desktop application maintained by Sniper Price Action Work.

1. Information handled on your computer

AutoClip is designed to run on your computer, not as a hosted video-processing service. Source media, transcripts, clips, exports, settings, and job history are stored locally by the app. Sniper Price Action Work does not receive this material through AutoClip's normal local workflow.

2. Optional AI providers

If you choose an external AI provider for clip selection, AutoClip sends transcript text to the provider you configured. It does not send the source video or audio to that provider for this feature. If you use local processing and a local model, that processing stays on your computer. The provider's own privacy terms apply to information sent to it.

3. TikTok and YouTube connections

If you connect an account, AutoClip uses the platform's authorization flow. OAuth credentials and account tokens are saved locally through your operating system's credential store when available. If no credential store is available, AutoClip may save them in its local configuration file and displays a warning. AutoClip requests TikTok's basic profile and video publishing permissions, and YouTube's video upload permission.

When you choose to publish, AutoClip sends the selected video and posting details directly to the platform you selected. TikTok or YouTube then handles that information under its own privacy policy. You can disconnect the account in AutoClip; you can also revoke access from the platform's account settings.

4. Website hosting

This information site is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as connection data when you visit the site under its privacy statement. The site does not intentionally set its own analytics or advertising cookies.

5. Retention and deletion

Local media, job data, settings, and credentials remain on your device until you remove them using the app or your operating system. Disconnecting an account removes the saved account token from AutoClip; removing platform credentials is a separate action in Settings. This does not delete data already held by TikTok, YouTube, or an AI provider.

6. Children and policy changes

AutoClip is a general-purpose desktop tool and is not directed to children. This policy may be updated as the app changes; the current version and its update date will be posted here.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the GitHub profile.
