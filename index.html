AutoClip

Turn long videos into short, captioned clips on your own computer. Review every clip, then choose whether to publish it to your connected TikTok or YouTube account.

View the open-source project Contact the developer

Work locally

Video processing runs on your computer. You can use local speech recognition and a local language model, or choose an outside AI provider.

Review before posting

Choose clips, edit captions, and export a finished video. Uploading to a connected platform happens only when you choose to publish.

Your accounts stay yours

AutoClip uses official TikTok and YouTube authorization flows. You can disconnect an account and remove saved credentials in the app.
